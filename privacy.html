Privacy Policy

App operator: Kashif Butt (individual developer)
Application: DocKey: Document Wallet

Last updated: October 17, 2026

DocKey helps you organize, scan and access family documents. We design DocKey to minimize transmission of sensitive information and keep document values on your device.

Information you choose to save

When you add a document, the app may process images, PDFs, text and extracted fields, including names, document numbers and expiry dates. The iPhone app uses on-device document recognition and encrypted local retention. The web app processes supported files in your browser and stores records in encrypted browser storage.

Automatic field-label matching

DocKey may transmit approved English field labels and a document type to a server-side OpenAI-powered matching service. Document values, full extracted text, images and PDFs are not sent for this purpose. DocKey is designed not to log AI request contents. The service provider's applicable processing terms and data controls apply.

Encrypted device-to-web sync

Optional sync uses end-to-end encryption. The relay temporarily handles encrypted payloads, pairing-session metadata and public keys rather than plaintext documents. Temporary sessions expire, and completed sessions are deleted. Copies saved in your browser remain in local browser storage until deleted or cleared.

Notifications

If enabled, expiry reminders are scheduled locally based on document dates you save. You control notification permissions in device settings.

Analytics and advertising

The documented current app has no analytics SDK, and it is not designed to use document content for behavioral advertising, profiling or model training, or to sell user data. Service operation may involve hosting or network metadata; deployed logging and third-party handling must be verified against the release build.

Retention and deletion

You can delete locally saved documents in DocKey. Deletions may synchronize through deletion markers to your other synced devices. Clearing local browser data may remove the browser's saved copy. Temporary relay data automatically expires.

Security

iPhone saved data uses local encryption and Keychain protections; browser storage uses WebCrypto-based encryption. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

Family members

Only add personal documents for people you are authorized to assist. DocKey is not intended for children to independently register accounts.

Changes to this policy

We may update this notice when features or data practices change. The current version will be published here.
